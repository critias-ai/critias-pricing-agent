# Pricing context: Halden Supply (fictional test data)
Scope: all files in this folder
Last updated: 2026-10-01

## Data
- Halden Supply is a fictional industrial distributor; this data is synthetic. All amounts are in GBP. [confirmed 2026-10-01; finance]
- invoices.csv has one row per invoice line; invoiced value = line_value. Quarters are calendar quarters. [confirmed 2026-10-01; finance]
- Net revenue = invoiced value − credit notes − rebates. An invoice counts in the quarter of its invoice date and a credit note in the quarter of its credit_date. A rebate counts in the quarter it was earned for (period), not the quarter it was paid. [confirmed 2026-10-01; finance]
