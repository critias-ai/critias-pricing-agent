# Sample data 1 live evaluation — 7–8 October 2026

**Completed exploratory evaluation. All 45 planned original messages have been attempted; recoveries are separately labeled.**

The dataset is suitable for testing brief prompts and realistic multi-turn pricing reviews. The completed supported calculations generally reconcile, and follow-ups retain the intended SKU, quarter, measure and ranking. This model/configuration still has material gaps in reliable completion, financial-pool validation, claim accuracy and consistent chart presentation. These exploratory results do not establish release readiness.

The suite has 18 cases and 45 original messages, including scripted clarification replies. Three separate confirmed-context conversations repeat a six-turn path: product group → top ten by revenue → SKU Q2 diagnosis → next-quarter recovery → lowest-margin top-ten item → latest-quarter re-ranking. A fourth conversation starts without confirmed context. Further cases test brief group/SKU/price requests and deliberately difficult data. Confirmed conversation 1 additionally changes adjustment treatment, includes an incomplete quarter and switches category. Those three branches are **serial**: later messages inherit the earlier allocation hold and fixed Q3 ranking; they are not independent forks.

Confirmed context defines the fictional reporting date, calendar window, signed invoice revenue, historical GBP/EA cost rules and pre-adjustment weighted invoice margin. This tests whether brief follow-ups reuse known definitions. Fresh-context cases separately test intake/clarification; their first replies are not expected to invent a financial definition. The fresh Lighting clarification originally omitted an explicit EA convention for blank `85123A.selling_uom`; its recovery supplies that evaluator fixture convention without resolving conflicting cost labels. It also asks for ranking earlier than the original group prompt. Do not compare the earlier unsupported population to fully confirmed results without that qualification.

All four initial Lighting group calculations exceeded the initial 12-minute allowance before a completed analysis answer. Recoveries can deliver correct reports, but this is a significant problem for interactive use. Completed top-ten analyses in the confirmed repeats took approximately 12.2, 13.8 and 19.2 minutes under the longer continuation allowance. Correct answers alone do not make this a clean usability pass.

Initial runs used a 12-minute per-turn allowance; continuation/recovery used 30 minutes, including subsequent original follow-ups. Some recorded wall times exceeded those allowances during interruptions/cancellation delays. Later runs contain clustered provider request timeouts/connection errors; the resumed wave also recorded five-minute request timeouts before some requests recovered. Record these as delivery failures/interruption evidence without claiming a verified root cause or attributing all elapsed time to model computation.

## Concrete defects and qualifications

1. **Service pool currency and duplicate-event validation.** Confirmed conversation 1 turn 8 quotes **£47,067.24** as variable service cost. That sums **£47,066.1535** in GBP-flagged rows with **EUR 1.0840**, despite the misleading `cost_amount_gbp` field name. The EUR record is `SVC-7D7F35A8EF4CF1B9`, invoice 568529. The GBP observed-row subtotal also contains two identical £3.80 OUTBOUND_DELIVERY payloads on invoice 568409 (`SVC-0D0B705E07608DE6` / `SVC-278048296791128A`). They share the dictionary’s documented business key (`cost_event_id` plus `cost_type`) and identical non-ID payloads, validating an analysis-view duplicate collapse. Do not deduplicate every `cost_event_id` alone, which groups multiple legitimate cost types. The comparable deduplicated GBP subtotal is £47,062.3535, with EUR 1.0840 still separate. The quoted pool is unsupported even though SKU allocation is correctly held. The £892.03 rebate amount does reconcile as Q3-reporting-month provisional annual-period movements, not a final quarter rebate obligation.
2. **Overbroad finance claim.** The fresh group recovery says all 15 monthly finance totals reconcile, but its bridge checks line count, signed quantity and invoice revenue after conditionally quarantining 1,346 classification-conflict rows. It does not establish full COGS, GP, adjustments, net sales or pocket reconciliation. Narrow the claim to the measures actually checked.
3. **Render assurance contradicts evidence.** The fresh group response claims no material clipping, while narrow previews clip internal chart titles and hide key right-side table fields. Several other final reports share these layout issues; ten-line legends overlap quarter-axis text. Toys' 49.5% peak label is cut at the top.
4. **Clarification burden and partial delivery.** Unknown X/Z and missing-period replies avoid fabricated results, but secondary metric/allocation questions can wait until identity/period is resolved. The Kitchen first reply says data ends in November; it actually includes partial December through the 9th. The “include current quarter” branch acknowledges a safe partial-period basis but repeats the allocation question without showing already-supported revenue.
